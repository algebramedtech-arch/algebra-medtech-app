App setup and next steps

  • This is a working prototype with mobile-friendly screens.
  • Product and anatomy content is illustrative; no licensed 3D model is included.
  • Visit notes are stored locally and do not sync across devices.
  • For installation, publish these files on an HTTPS host, open the URL in Chrome, then use ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
  • For production: add staff login, role permissions, cloud sync, backup, licensed 3D anatomy and approved product documents.
This app is not a medical decision-support tool. Follow applicable medical-device, privacy, security and manufacturer requirements before business deployment.
